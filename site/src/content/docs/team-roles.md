Invite teammates by email under **Settings, Team**. The number of team members depends on your plan.

## Roles

| | Owner | Admin | Viewer |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| See servers, websites and alerts | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Change alerts, contacts, servers and websites | Yes | Yes | No |
| Invite and remove team members | Yes | Yes | No |
| Manage the API key | Yes | Yes | No |
| Billing and plan | Yes | No | No |
| Delete the organization or transfer ownership | Yes | No | No |

## How invites work

1. You enter an email address and a role.
2. We email a link that is valid for 7 days and works once.
3. A new person sets their name and password. Someone who already has an account clicks **Join**. The email address must match the invite.
4. You can resend or cancel a pending invite at any time.

## Removing someone

Remove them in **Settings, Team**. Their access ends straight away. The Owner cannot be removed, and you cannot remove yourself.
